Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026. This site is a small, free PDF tools site. The short version: your PDF files never leave your device. The tools run entirely in your browser, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not store your files.

1. Your files stay on your device

Merge PDF, Split PDF and Compress PDF run 100% in your browser, using the open-source pdf-lib engine (and JSZip for ZIP downloads on the Split page, and pdf.js for reading pages on the Compress page). When you add a PDF, it is read locally, processed on your device, and the result is created in your browser tab for you to download.

Your files are never uploaded to a server, never sent to us, and never stored by us. There is no account, no sign-up, and no file history. When you close the tab, nothing about your files remains on this site.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, see, or store:

  • Your PDF files, their contents, file names, or page counts
  • Your name, address, or phone number
  • Any account or login information — there is no account system on this site

The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us yourself — for example, if you email us, we will see your email address and whatever you write in that message. We use it only to reply to you.

3. Cookies and advertising

This site does not use analytics, and it does not show ads yet. Google’s AdSense code is on the site so Google can verify it, but no ads are shown while the site is still under review.

We plan to apply for Google AdSense so that the site can show ads and stay free. If Google approves this site, Google AdSense and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies to show ads on this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Until ads are actually shown, no advertising cookies are set by this site itself. If and when advertising is added, this policy will be updated to say so.

4. Third-party services that load on this site

This site loads a few third-party resources so the tools can work:

  • Google Fonts — for the site’s fonts.
  • jsDelivr — for the pdf-lib code that merges and splits PDFs in your browser.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — for the JSZip code that creates ZIP downloads on the Split page, and the pdf.js code that reads pages on the Compress page.

When your browser loads those resources, those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as described in their own privacy policies. We do not control those providers, and we do not send them your files or any information about you.

5. Children’s privacy

This site is a general-audience PDF tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add analytics or advertising. When we do, we will change the effective date above. The version published on this page is the current one.
