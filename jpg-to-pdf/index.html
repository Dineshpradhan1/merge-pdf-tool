    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is it really free? ＋

    Yes. Conversion runs in your browser, so there is no server bill to recover — no account, no trial, no watermark, and no daily limit set by this page. Very large photos work best within your device's memory.

    Are my photos uploaded anywhere? ＋

    No. Your images are read locally, placed into a PDF on your device, and the result is created in this tab for download. Nothing leaves your device.

    Which image types work? ＋

    JPG (and JPEG) and PNG. Other files — such as PDFs, Word files, or videos — cannot be converted here and will be skipped with a message telling you so.

    Will my photos lose quality? ＋

    No. Your photos are placed into the PDF as they are, at their original quality. The PDF keeps the quality of the original image, so a small or blurry photo will stay small or blurry — but a sharp photo stays sharp.

    Can I change the order of the photos? ＋

    Yes. Drag a photo card to move it, or use the up and down arrows on each card. The PDF pages follow the order shown on the page.

    What page size should I choose? ＋

    A4 is the best choice for printing and for forms in India. Letter is the US standard. “Fit to image” makes each page the same shape as its photo, which is best for wide or tall photos you do not want cropped.
