    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is it really free? ＋

    Yes. Splitting runs in your browser, so there is no server bill to recover — no account, no trial, no watermark, and no daily limit set by this page. Very large files work best under about 100 MB total, limited only by your device's memory.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere? ＋

    No. Your PDF is read locally, split on your device, and the results are created in this tab for download. Nothing leaves your device.

    How do I write page ranges? ＋

    Use numbers and dashes, separated by commas — for example 1-3, 5, 8-10. Each range becomes its own PDF file. Page numbers start at 1, and every page must be inside your PDF: if your file has 10 pages, 11 is not a valid page.

    What if I split every page of a long PDF? ＋

    Every page becomes its own PDF, and they download together as one ZIP file so you only download once. A 100-page PDF makes 100 small files in that ZIP — that is normal, and it works best on a computer for very long files.

    What if my PDF shows an error? ＋

    That PDF is likely password-protected or damaged. Remove it, unlock or repair it, then add it again. This tool cannot open password-protected PDFs.
