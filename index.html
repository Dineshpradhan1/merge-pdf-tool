    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is it really free? ＋

    Yes. Because merging runs in your browser, there's no server bill to recover — no account, no trial, no watermark, no 2-tasks-a-day cap, and no file-count cap set by this page. Very large batches work best under about 100 MB total, limited only by your device's memory.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere? ＋

    No. Files are read locally, merged on your device, and the result is created in this tab for download. Nothing leaves your device.

    How many PDFs can I merge at once? ＋

    No hard cap. Dozens of normal documents merge fine. The practical limit is your device's memory — very large scans (100 MB+) will just take a little longer.

    How do I reorder files? ＋

    Drag a card by the ⠿ handle on desktop, or use the ↑ ↓ buttons on any card — easiest on mobile. The list order is the merged order.

    What if a file shows an error? ＋

    That PDF is likely password-protected or damaged. Remove it with ✕, unlock or repair it, then add it again. The rest of your files are unaffected.

    Will quality drop after merging? ＋

    No — pages are copied without recompression, so the output matches the originals exactly.
