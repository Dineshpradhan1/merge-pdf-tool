About

Free PDF tools, made simple and private.

Merge PDF Tool is a small, free website for everyday PDF jobs. No accounts, no sign-up, no watermark, and no waiting for a server — the tools run 100% in your browser, so your files are never uploaded and never stored.

Why this site exists

Most PDF tools ask you to upload your documents to a server, create an account, or pay before you can do anything. We wanted the opposite: open the page, do the job, download the result, and close the tab. Nothing about your files stays behind.

That is why the tools are built on the open-source pdf-lib engine, which runs entirely in your browser. Merging and splitting happen on your own device — not on a server somewhere else.

Merge PDF →

Combine many PDFs into one file, in your exact order. Free, private, and done in under a minute.

Split PDF →

Split a PDF by page range, save every page as its own file, or extract only the pages you need.

What you can expect

Free. Both tools are free to use, with no file-count cap set by this site and no daily limit.

Private. Your files are never uploaded, never stored, and never seen by us. Close the tab and nothing is stored anywhere.

Simple. Drop a file, choose what to do, download the result. It works on phone and computer, with nothing to install.
